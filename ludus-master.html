The game

You don't play the gladiator. You play the man who owns him.

Capua, 71 BC. A legate rigged a contract, buried you in debt and put your best man in an arena he should never have entered.

Buy men at the market, break them, forge them, and send them to die in front of the crowd. Every victory becomes coin, reputation and favour; every defeat costs a man — and the men you lose, you lose for good.

The rules

Three rules. The whole game is in them.

01

The market

Men are cheap, good men are not. Each one comes with five statistics, a school and a temper — and a man who dies in the arena does not come back.

02

The forge

A trident or a steel cuirass — this season or the next. The net holds the distance, the scythe sweeps its arc, the twin blades never stop.

03

The colosseum

A new card of bouts every week: a safe duel, a man against a beast, one against two. Pick one, set your wager, and cheat from the stands.

The arena

Then you go down to the sand yourself.

Fights are played in real time, with the controls of a MOBA. Four schools, each with four skills that set each other up — the order of your keys is the whole game.

Retiarius

The net snares — the long thrust becomes the Harpoon.

Secutor

The shield charge stuns — the short thrust becomes the Nail.

Thraex

The cut makes him bleed — the scythe reopens the wound, all at once.

Murmillo

A feint or a clean parry exposes him — the heavy blow becomes the Crush.
