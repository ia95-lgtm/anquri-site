0 % The game

You don't play
the gladiator. You
play the man who owns him.

Capua, 71 BC. A legate rigged a contract, buried you in debt and put your best man in an arena he should never have entered. You have some sand, a few slaves and three seasons to rebuild.

Buy men at the market, break them, forge them, and send them to die in front of the crowd. Every victory becomes coin, reputation and favour; every defeat costs a man — and the men you lose, you lose for good. At the end of the third season, if one of your champions is still standing, you go after the legate.

25 % The ludus

Every week, the purse
decides who fights.

The market

Men are cheap, good men are not. Each one comes with five statistics, a school and a temper — and a man who dies in the arena does not come back, however much you paid for him.

The forge

A trident or a steel cuirass — this season or the next. Weapons change how a man fights: the net and the trident hold the distance, the scythe sweeps everyone in its arc, the twin blades never stop.

The colosseum

A new card of bouts every week: a safe duel, a man against a beast, one against two. Pick one, set your wager, and cheat from the stands — the other master is doing the same.

50 % The arena

Then you go
down to the sand
yourself.

The fights are played in real time, with the controls of a MOBA: right-click to move, the basic attack locks onto your target, and four skills go where your cursor points.

Four schools — retiarius, secutor, thraex, murmillo — each with four skills that set each other up. One skill puts the target in a state; another one exploits it, hits harder, and gives back part of the first skill's cooldown. The order of your keys is the whole game.

  • RetiariusThe net snares — the long thrust becomes the Harpoon.
  • SecutorThe shield charge stuns — the short thrust becomes the Nail.
  • ThraexThe cut makes him bleed — the scythe reopens the wound, all at once.
  • MurmilloA feint or a clean parry exposes him — the heavy blow becomes the Crush.

Combos have names

When a combo lands, the world stops for a beat and its name is written over the target. That is how you learn the order of your keys: by seeing it pay.

Read the floor

Bosses draw their attacks on the sand before they fall: circles that roll after you, volleys of pila that stay planted, rings you must step out of. Nothing hits you without warning — but a lot of it hits hard.

Bosses don't wait

A boss in the middle of a skill cannot be stunned. It chains its moves, punishes you for keeping your distance, and every phase opens with a roar that throws everyone around it back.

75 % The trial of the sand

His own house,
room by room.

The last fight is not in the arena. It is in the legate's villa, at night — his kennels, his great hall, his palaestra — with his guard, his standard-bearer and his champion standing between you and him.

He runs when it turns bad. Cut him off before he reaches the door, and the crowd gets the only ending it came for.
