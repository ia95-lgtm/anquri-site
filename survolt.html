The game

A ball that is worth more every time it changes hands.

It starts white. One completed pass turns it cyan and doubles it. When all three of you have touched it, it burns gold and triples it. Hold it too long and it melts back down a step.

Every shot scores something. What matters is how much.

The rules

Three rules. The whole game is in them.

01

The charge is the score

White, cyan, gold: ×1, ×2, ×3. The screen shows you one thing — the colour of the ball — and every decision in a possession is a bet on it.

02

A target, not a goal

The targets are dartboards bolted to the end walls, and they move. The outer ring scores one, the bullseye three — times the charge. Bank shots count.

03

The gold zone

Now and then a patch of the arena lights up. Score from it, or bounce the ball off it, and the shot counts double. In the last minute everything doubles again: Overdrive.

The moves

Dodge it, hit it, then light it up.

Every athlete is built in the game's own editor — body, hair, outfit — and takes to the pitch in the SURVOLT suit, in the team's colour.

The 360

A tackle is coming and you have the ball? Spin. Time it right and the tackle hits nothing — and the dodge charges your aura.

Shoulder or slide

Defenders take the ball back with a shoulder charge or a sliding tackle, and cut passes out of the air with a jump or a dive.

The aura

Passes, interceptions, tackles, screens and dodges fill a gauge. Full, it lights your aura for eight seconds: faster runs, heavier shots, volleys that find the target.

Thirty-six is a bullseye with a gold ball, from the gold zone, during Overdrive. And the zero is the point of the online mode: nobody touches their router — the studio's server introduces the players, and relays the match when a network is too strict.

Online

Find a match, or bring your friends.

Public games sorted by real ping, private games behind a six-letter code, quick play if you just want to be on the pitch. Empty seats are played by the AI, and everything works with a gamepad.
