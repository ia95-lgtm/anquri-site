900 K The game

Fast shooters need
an engine that
forbids waiting.

This one uses thermodynamics. Your core heats up when you fire, and the only heat sink in the game is the air moving over your body.

The consequence needs no tutorial: take cover to shoot in peace, and you melt in eight seconds. There is no other rule to learn, and everything below is a consequence of this one.

1400 K The consequences

Three rules, and the
whole game is in them.

The red zone pays

Damage scales with your own temperature. At 92 % you hit nearly twice as hard — so the economy of the game pushes you to the edge of an explosion and keeps you there.

Colour is health

Nothing here has a texture. An enemy's colour is its health: dark red when intact, white hot a moment before it dies. You never read a health bar. You read the room.

The ground is where you burn

Standing on solid ground dissipates nothing at all, and the faster you fly the harder you cool. Every weapon's recoil is a thruster. Shooting is moving.

1700 K The bosses

Every boss
plays to
the music.

Each one fights on the beat of its own synthesised theme, shows you every attack one beat before it lands, and makes compulsory a rule the rest of the game only rewards.

Bring down the fifth and the music stops. What it was holding at the bottom comes out. That is all we will say.

LE CREUSET
A casting ladle hanging from its chains. It only opens in the red zone — and its quench steam keeps dragging you out of it.
LE MARTELEUR
A steam hammer that walks. Every blow cracks the floor open with molten iron: don't be standing on it.
LE CONVERTISSEUR
It turns your own shots against you, then confiscates the weapon you use most.
LE PUITS
The heat sink the game is named after. It swallows your heat, and only a deliberate overload cracks it open.
LE PATRON
Your mirror: your weapons, your heat rules. You beat him by making him overheat.
