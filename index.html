The studio

Small studio.
Big ideas.

ANQURI is one developer and a lot of ideas. Every game starts from something we haven't seen anywhere else — and we follow it as far as it goes.

The heat in HEATSINK is not a mechanic bolted onto a shooter — it is the shooter. SURVOLT is built the same way around a ball that is worth more every time it changes hands; SAINT-MARC around a nurse who cannot bear her own face; LUDUS MASTER around a school where nothing you lose ever comes back. Everything is made with Godot, and the studio runs its own game server.
