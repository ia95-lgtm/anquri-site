900 K the game

One gauge.
No health bar, no ammo, no timer.

Fast shooters all need an engine that forbids waiting. This one uses thermodynamics. Your core heats up when you fire, and the only heat sink in the game is the air moving over your body.

The consequence needs no tutorial: take cover to shoot in peace, and you melt in eight seconds.

1400 K three rules, and the whole game is in them

The red zone pays

Damage scales with your own temperature. At 92 % you hit nearly twice as hard — so the economy of the game pushes you to the edge of an explosion and keeps you there.

Colour is health

Nothing here has a texture. An enemy's colour is its health: dark red when intact, white hot a moment before it dies. You never read a health bar. You read the room.

The ground is where you burn

Standing on solid ground dissipates nothing at all, and the faster you fly the harder you cool. Every weapon's recoil is a thruster. Shooting is moving.

3 modes 6 weapons 22 enemy species 5 bosses 12 movement courses 7 languages 0 audio files

That last number is not a typo. Every sound effect and all six music tracks are synthesised while the game runs — there is not one audio file in the build, and not one texture either.
